## Team Collaboration Model

### Weekly Cadence
- **Monday**: Team planning meeting & weekly goals
- **Wednesday**: Mid-week check-in & blocker resolution
- **Friday**: End-of-week review & demo of completed work

### Integration Points
1. **Data Layer → Business Logic**
   - Alex provides database interfaces to Taylor
   - Regular sync meetings to ensure alignment

2. **Business Logic → UI**
   - Taylor exposes system functions to Jamie
   - Collaborative sessions for interface design

3. **System-wide Testing**
   - Morgan coordinates with all team members
   - Regular testing cycles with structured feedback

## Communication Channels
- **Technical Decisions**: Team discussion board
- **Quick Questions**: Team chat channel
- **Code Review**: Shared repository with pull request process
- **Documentation**: Shared document workspace

## Future Enhancement Areas
- **Phase 5** (Future Release): Multi-user support with authentication
- **Phase 6** (Future Release): Advanced reporting and analytics

## Team Success Principles
1. **Collaborative Ownership**: Every team member understands the full system
2. **Clear Communication**: Regular updates and documentation of decisions
3. **User-Centered Development**: All features designed with the end-user in mind
4. **Continuous Improvement**: Regular retrospectives and adaptation
5. **Knowledge Sharing**: Cross-training sessions to build team expertise
